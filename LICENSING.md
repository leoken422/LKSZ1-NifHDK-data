# Licensing and source attribution

Assemblies and protein records have been collected from multiple supplied sources. Original accessions and biological metadata are retained in the manifest where available. Original source licence information was not supplied for the individual assemblies. This preparation does not assert ownership of third-party records or apply a new blanket licence to them.

The Figshare depositor must select a licence appropriate for the materials being deposited. Original source attribution and applicable source terms should accompany reused data. The newly prepared metadata, documentation, and verification utility can be assigned their own reuse terms by the depositing authors. No project-wide licence has been assigned during local preparation.

Accession links and unresolved-source tables are provided to support source attribution. Where no accession or source publication was supplied, those fields remain unresolved rather than being inferred from the genome filename.
